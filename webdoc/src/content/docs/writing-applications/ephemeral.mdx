Ephemeral queues live in memory, outside the log, for request/reply. The worker answers on whatever inbox a request names; the caller makes a private inbox and waits on it. There is no transaction or replay here, and a crash loses what is in flight.

<Why>a request that waits for an answer ("what is the price of A-1, now?") needs speed more than history. Writing it to disk, replicating it and keeping it for days would cost time for nothing. Ephemeral queues keep the same groups, leases and acks, and skip the log. Use a regular queue for anything that must survive a crash.</Why>
