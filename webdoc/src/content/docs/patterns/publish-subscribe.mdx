One event, many readers: when an order is placed, billing charges it, shipping prepares it, and analytics counts it. Each reader must get every event, at its own pace, and a reader added later must be able to start from the beginning.

Each reader is a consumer group. A group gets every message of the queue and keeps its own position; the workers inside a group share its work, as in a work queue.

<Fig id="pubsub" caption="Published once, read by every group, also by one that joins later." />

<SdkCode id="uc-pubsub" />

Billing and shipping each got the three orders. Analytics, started later, read all three from the start.

<Why>the queue is a log: reading does not delete. So a new subscriber costs nothing to the publisher, and a group that falls behind slows down only itself.</Why>

One thing to know: a new group starts at the end of the queue unless it asks for `subscriptionMode('all')`, or a point in time with `subscriptionFrom`. Our check proved it: a group created without it found nothing.
