The booking page needs a price from the pricing service, now. If no answer comes in half a second, it shows "ask us". A durable queue is too heavy for that, and a direct HTTP call ties the two services together.

Ephemeral queues live in memory, outside the log. The caller makes a private inbox, sends it as `replyTo`, and waits for the answer with a timeout. Any instance of the pricing service can answer.

<Fig id="request-reply" caption="A quote with a deadline: the answer, or &quot;ask us&quot;." />

<SdkCode id="uc-reply" />

The answer came back in time: `{room: 'deluxe', price: 129}`. With no pricing service running, the caller gave up after half a second and got `price: null`.

<Why>an answer that comes too late is worth nothing, so it does not need a disk. In memory a round trip costs very little, and the service scales like any consumer group.</Why>

One thing to know: a node that crashes loses the ephemeral messages it held. There are no transactions, dead letters or replays on these queues.
