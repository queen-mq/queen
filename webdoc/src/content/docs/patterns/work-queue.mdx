Many workers share one stream of jobs, and each job is done by exactly one of them: resizing photos, sending emails, rendering invoices. This is the work queue, also called competing consumers.

In Queen it is a consumer with no consumer group. Each message goes to one worker. Work runs in parallel across partitions: a partition is held by one worker at a time, so here every user's photos are a partition, and three workers share four users.

<Fig id="work-queue" caption="Competing consumers: each job once, one user's partition per worker at a time." />

<SdkCode id="uc-work" />

All twelve photos were resized once each, shared out between the three workers (6, 3 and 3 in one run).

<Why>order still holds inside each user's photos, and a slow user holds up only its own partition. To go faster, add workers, up to the number of partitions that have work.</Why>

One thing to know: with a single partition the workers would take turns, one at a time. Give the jobs a partition per user, per customer or per tenant.
