digest: >-
  The Queen broker is an HTTP/JSON API on port 6632 of every node; a follower forwards /api/ and
  /streams/ requests to the leader, so a client may call any node. POST /api/v1/transaction takes
  `operations` (ack and push) plus top-level `kv`, `timers` and `positions` arrays and answers HTTP
  200 for a commit (`success: true`) and for a rollback (`success: false` with a `reason`).
  Engine errors are `{"error", "code"}`: 503 `retry`, `no_leader` or `timeout` mean retry,
  503 `outcome_unknown` means an ack may or may not have applied, 429 `overloaded` and 507
  `storage_full` mean wait. The broker is configured only by environment variables, exposes
  Prometheus metrics on GET /metrics/prometheus, and accepts Kafka clients through a built-in
  wire-protocol facade (QUEEN_KAFKA_EMBEDDED=true).
