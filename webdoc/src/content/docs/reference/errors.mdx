When Queen refuses a call it answers a status code and a JSON body with a stable code, and the code
is the part your client should branch on: messages get reworded, codes do not. Most of the
decisions a client has to make come down to three questions, and the tables below answer them for
every code: can I retry this as it is, do I have to fix the request, or do I need to find out
whether it already happened.

A rolled-back transaction is not an error. It answers 200 with `success: false` and a `reason`, and
those reasons are on [transaction](/reference/transaction/#rollback-reasons).

## Error bodies

| Where | Body | Branch on |
|---|---|---|
| Most routes: push, pop, ack, lease, queues, consumer groups, DLQ, status | `{"error": "<message>", "code": "<code>"}`, plus `"leader"` on a 503 `retry` when the leader is known | `code` |
| Reads by offset refused for their shape (too many entries, a negative offset, a bad timestamp) | `{"error": "<message>"}` with 400; a per-entry `error` (`UNKNOWN_TOPIC_OR_PARTITION`, `OFFSET_OUT_OF_RANGE`, `BAD_CURSOR`) inside a 200 | the status, then each entry |
| `POST /api/v1/transaction`, rolled back | `{"transactionId", "success": false, "reason", "error", "results": []}` | `reason` |
| `/api/v1/kv*` and `/api/v1/timers*` | `{"error": "<code>", "reason": "<finer code>", "detail": "<message>"}` | `error`, then `reason` |
| `/api/v1/ephemeral/*` | `{"error": "<message>", "code": "<code>"}` | `code` |
| A pop refused for its parameters | `{"success": false, "error": "<message>", "messages": []}` with 400 | the status |
| Authentication and the tenancy header | `{"error": "<message>"}` | the status |
| The proxy, and `/api/cp` | `{"error": "<message>", "code": "<code>"}` | `code` |
| Raft membership changes | `{"ok": false, "code": "<code>", "error": "<message>"}` | `code` |

The 429 and 503 answers of the engine, KV and timer routes carry `Retry-After`, in seconds. A 404
for something that does not exist (a queue, a message) may carry only `error`.

## Engine codes

The `code` of the first shape.

| `code` | Status | Meaning | Client |
|---|---|---|---|
| `retry` | 503 | This node could not get the call to a leader: a leader change, no quorum lease, or the leader unreachable from the node that forwarded it. | Retry after `Retry-After` (1 s). A write may have reached the leader, so it must be safe to repeat. |
| `no_leader` | 503 | No leader is known after the hold: an election is running, or a majority is down. | Retry. |
| `timeout` | 503 | The request's deadline passed before an answer. A write may have committed. | Retry a write that is safe to repeat; otherwise read to check. |
| `outcome_unknown` | 503 | The leader changed while an ack, nack or DLQ move was being made durable, or a forwarded one was lost after it went out. It may or may not have applied. | Send the same ack again: a repeat of an ack that applied is answered as the first one was. |
| `overloaded` | 429 | The node's admission budget for writes stayed full for the whole hold. | Retry after `Retry-After` (1 to 5 s). |
| `storage_full` | 507 | A node is above its disk high-water mark (`QUEEN_RAFT_DISK_HIGH_PCT`, 85%). Pushes, KV writes and timer schedules are refused; acks and deletes still work. | Free disk or add space, then retry. |
| `name_too_long` | 413 | The tenant, queue and partition or group names together exceed the store's key budget. | Shorten the name. |
| `bad_body` | 400 | The body is not the route's JSON shape. | Fix the request. |
| `bad_request` | 400 | A field is missing or invalid. | Fix the request. |
| any other code | 400 | The engine refused this request for the reason the message gives. | Fix the request; do not retry it unchanged. |
| `no_such_route` | 404 | No route has that method and path. | Check the path against [the routes](/reference/http/#routes). |
| `internal` | 500 | A broker fault. | Report it; do not retry in a loop. |
| `raft_phase1_unsupported` | 503 | The node does not serve this call. | Report it. |

**A 503 or a timeout on a write does not mean nothing happened.** Make writes safe to repeat
before you retry them: a deterministic `transactionId` on every pushed message, the same lease on a
repeated ack, a `required` marker in a transaction. See [dedup](/concepts/dedup/).

## After a leader change

What a leader change does to a call in flight depends on where the call is remembered.

Pushes, transactions, KV and timer calls are logged under a request id that the node serving the
call minted. That node follows the leader change itself and resubmits under the same id until the
deadline, and a command that had already committed is answered from its record and does not run
again. You get the normal answer, or 503 `timeout` if the deadline passes first.

Acks, nacks and DLQ moves are served from the leader's memory and made durable by its next
checkpoint, a few milliseconds later. If the leader loses its lead while that checkpoint is on its
way to the log, or a forwarded ack is lost after it went out, nobody can say whether it applied, and
the answer is 503 `outcome_unknown`. Send the same ack, with the same lease, again.

Pops are never in doubt: a claim nobody receives is handed back or ends with its lease, and its
messages are delivered again. A node that cannot reach the leader answers 503 `retry` or
`no_leader`, and the request may have reached the leader first.

All of this holds for a cluster. Lease exclusivity across a leader change assumes node clocks
within `QUEEN_RAFT_MAX_CLOCK_SKEW_MS` (500 ms) of each other. See
[guarantees](/concepts/guarantees/).

## KV and timer codes

The `error` of the third shape. The `reason` beside it is finer, and stable too.

| `error` | Status | `reason`, and when | Client |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kv_bad_request` | 400 | `kv_bad_body`, `kv_bad_namespace`, `kv_bad_key`, `kv_expiry_not_specified`, `kv_bad_ttl`, `kv_bad_expect`, `kv_unknown_op`, `kv_prefix_required`, `kv_too_many_ops`, `kv_too_many_keys`, `kv_duplicate_key_in_call`, `kv_tenant_not_an_input`, `kv_no_query_string`, `kv_field_in_path`. | Fix the request. |
| `timers_bad_request` | 400 | `timers_bad_body`, `timers_bad_op`, `timers_queue_required`, `timers_delay_required`, `timers_payload_required`, `timers_payload_not_base64`, `timers_too_many_ops`, `timers_server_owned_field`, or a read-mode reason on the list routes. | Fix the request. |
| `payload_too_large` | 413 | `kv_value_too_large`, `kv_key_too_large`, `timers_payload_too_large`, `timers_call_too_large`. | Shrink it, or raise the ceiling (see [limits](/reference/limits/)). |
| `timer_horizon_exceeded` | 403 | `timers_horizon`: `delayMs` is beyond the horizon in force. | Use a shorter delay. |
| `kv_quota_exceeded`, `timers_quota_exceeded` | 403 | The tenant is over its KV rows or bytes, or its timer count. Deletes and cancels are never refused for this. | Free space or raise the quota; do not retry unchanged. |
| `feature_gated` | 403 | The tenant has no grant for this surface (`QUEEN_KV_REQUIRE_GRANT`). | An operator grants it. |
| `rate_limited` | 429 | The tenant's KV read or write rate. | Retry after `Retry-After`. |
| `kv_unavailable`, `timers_unavailable` | 503 | `kv_timeout`, `kv_retry`, `kv_no_leader`, `timers_timeout`, `timers_retry`, `no_leader`; or `kv_unavailable` when the node already tracks `QUEEN_KV_MAX_TENANTS` (10,000) tenants. | Retry after `Retry-After`. |
| `kv_disabled`, `timers_disabled` | 503 | An operator paused the surface at runtime. | Retry later. |
| `kv_error`, `timers_error` | 500 | A broker fault. | Report it. |

A lost precondition on `POST /api/v1/kv` is not an error either. An ordinary one shows as
`applied: false` in its result, and a `required` one answers 200 with
`{"ok": false, "reason": "kv_precondition", "failedIndex", "kvReason", "version", "value"}`.
Cancelling a timer that is not pending answers 200 with `ok: false`.

## Ephemeral codes

| `code` | Status | Meaning | Client |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ephemeral_bad_request` | 400 | The request is malformed: a name, a batch, an id list. | Fix the request. |
| `ephemeral_queue_not_found` | 404 | No ephemeral queue by that name exists. | Check the name. |
| `queue_full` | 429 | The queue is at its `maxBytes` or `maxLength` and its overflow policy is reject. | Back off and push again once consumers drain it. |
| `rate_limited` | 429 | The tenant is over its ephemeral messages-per-second allowance. | Retry after `Retry-After`. |
| `ephemeral_quota_exceeded` | 403 | The tenant is at its ephemeral byte or queue allowance. | Drain, reset or delete a queue. |
| `feature_gated` | 403 | The tenant is not granted the ephemeral class (`QUEEN_EPHEMERAL_REQUIRE_GRANT`). | An operator grants it. |
| `ephemeral_unavailable` | 503 | This node is at `QUEEN_EPHEMERAL_MAX_BYTES`. | Retry later. |
| `ephemeral_disabled` | 503 | An operator paused the surface at runtime. | Retry after `Retry-After`. |
| `owner_moved`, `ephemeral_forward_failed` | 503 | The partition's owner changed while the call was relayed, or could not be reached. | Retry. |
| `internal_only` | 403 | A broker-to-broker route was called without the forward mark. | Not a client route. |

## Authentication and tenancy

| Status | `error` | Client |
|---|---|---|
| 401 | `Authentication required`, `Token has expired`, `Token not yet valid`, `Token issued in the future`, `Invalid token signature`, `Invalid token issuer`, `Invalid token audience`, `Unknown key ID`, `Bad token header`, `Unsupported token algorithm` | Get a valid token. |
| 403 | `Insufficient permissions` | Use a token whose role covers the route's access level ([HTTP API](/reference/http/#authentication)). |
| 400 | `invalid x-queen-tenant header (must be a UUID)` | Fix whatever sets the header (normally the proxy). |

## Proxy codes

Behind the embedded proxy, these answers come before the request reaches the broker. Plans and
limits are on [tenants](/operate/tenants/).

| `code` | Status | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `unauthorized` | 401 | No valid API key or session. |
| `forbidden` | 403 | The credential has no role on this cluster, or is for another cluster. |
| `rate_limited` | 429 | A request or message rate limit, with `Retry-After`. |
| `quota_exceeded` | 403 | A queue, partition or monthly message quota is reached. |
| `storage_quota_exceeded` | 403 | The retained-bytes quota is reached; pushes are blocked. |
| `push_blocked` | 403 | Pushes are blocked on this cluster (billing hold). |
| `cluster_suspended` | 403 | The cluster is suspended. |
| `feature_gated` | 403 | The route is not in the plan. |
| `route_blocked` | 404 | A broker route that is not served to tenants. |
| `cluster_unknown` | 421 | The `Host` names no cluster. |
| `payload_too_large` | 413 | The body is over the edge limit, an item's payload over the plan's `max_payload_bytes`, or a push over `max_batch_items`. |
| `bad_gateway`, `upstream_timeout` | 502, 504 | The broker did not answer. |

### Control-plane codes

The `/api/cp` routes answer the proxy envelope with these codes. With `QUEEN_PROXY_CP_TOKEN` unset
every route answers 404 `not_found`; a wrong token answers 401 `unauthorized`, and a body over
1 MiB 413 `payload_too_large`.

| `code` | Status | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `invalid` | 400 | A field, a slug or a UUID is not valid. |
| `conflict` | 409 | The record exists, or changed under the call. |
| `tenant_unknown`, `cluster_unknown`, `key_unknown` | 404 | No such tenant, cluster or key. |
| `not_deleting` | 409 | A purge or delete of a tenant whose status is not `deleting`. |
| `not_purged` | 409 | A delete of a tenant whose clusters still list queues at the broker. |
| `deleting` | 409 | A change to a tenant or cluster that is being torn down. |
| `system_tenant` | 409 | The call would act on the broker's default tenant or the proxy's own, or the tenant header is off. |
| `unavailable`, `no_store` | 503 | The proxy's state is not readable yet. Retry. |

## Raft membership codes

`/api/v1/system/raft/membership/*` answers a refused change with one of these codes: 400 for
`bad_request`, `not_a_learner` and `not_a_member`; 409 for `in_flight`, `no_quorum`, `last_voter`,
`learner_behind`, `already_voter`, `address_mismatch`, `membership_changed` and `leader_changed`;
503 for `leader_unreachable`; and 504 `timeout` when the deadline (`?timeoutMs=`, default 30 s)
passed, in which case the change may still complete, so read the membership before repeating it. A
request that carries a tenant answers 403 `forbidden`: the membership belongs to the whole cluster.
See [cluster](/operate/cluster/).

## What the JS client does

- It retries a 5xx or a network error: with one URL up to three attempts, waiting 1 s and then 2 s;
  with several URLs and failover on, it tries each URL once. Every 5xx is treated alike, 507 and 503
  `outcome_unknown` included.
- It retries a 429 in place after `Retry-After`, up to 10 attempts, and without limit for a
  long-poll pop.
- Any other 4xx is thrown at once, with `error.status` and `error.code` from the body.
- A transaction rolled back by a lost `required` precondition is returned from `commit()`; every
  other rollback is thrown with `error.reason` set.

Branch on the rollback reasons the broker sends, listed on
[transaction](/reference/transaction/#rollback-reasons). The reason list in the Rust protocol
crate (`crates/queen-protocol`) still names the 1.x codes `ack_rejected`, `misaligned` and
`db_error`, which 2.0 never sends: a fenced ack that fails is `rejected_ack`.
