Architecture · three nodes each

Kafka with KRaft and ISR. QueenMQ with one raft log.

Kafka · KRaft + ISRcombined mode: broker and controller on each node
  • Each partition: a leader, and followers that fetch from it. The caught-up ones are the ISR.
  • Metadata: a second raft log, among the controllers. Offsets: the topic __consumer_offsets.
  • Transactions: a coordinator, and markers in every partition.

QueenMQ · one binary, one raft logevery node holds a full copy
  • One leader orders all the tenant's writes. Followers prepare their clients' requests and answer from their copy.
  • One log: messages, acks, KV, timers. Positions and leases: in the leader's memory, checkpointed.
  • A transaction is one log entry: no coordinator, no markers.

Kafka spreads partition leaders over the nodes, so writes use all three. QueenMQ has one leader per tenant: more tenants (raft groups) spread the leaders.
