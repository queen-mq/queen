\chapter{Manage clusters}

A broker starts with no settings at all, and most installations change very few. These are the
ones that matter most. Some are read only once, when a node creates its data directory, so choose
them before the first start. The sections of this chapter explain each one where it is used.

% Draft by Claude, 2026-10-24. Defaults checked in server/src (config.rs, rsm/batcher.rs,
% rsm/maintenance.rs, rsm/retention_scan.rs) and reference/configuration.mdx.
\begin{options}{Setting}{Default}{What it does}
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\itshape Read only at the first start} \\
QUEEN\_QLOG\_SHARDS & 0 & \texttt{0}: each queue has its own log files. \texttt{K}: all queues share K logs, so a write syncs at most K files. For thousands of queues. \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_PEERS & unset & The nodes of a cluster (see Run a cluster with Docker). After the first start, members change only through the membership API. \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_DIR & /var/lib/\allowbreak queen/raft & The data directory, one per node. \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\itshape How fast old data leaves} \\
PARTITION\_CLEANUP\_SECONDS & 30 days & An empty partition is deleted after this long without a write. At least 60. \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_TXN\_WINDOW\_MIN\_S & 900 & The least time a message's dedup record is kept. Nothing is deleted sooner, partitions included. \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_RETENTION\_SCAN\_PER\_S & 50000 & Partitions the retention scan visits a second. On 2.6.0, set \texttt{40000} when partitions come and go fast; 2.7.0 keeps up without it (see Many short-lived partitions). \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_TRACE\_RETENTION\_S & 7 days & How long message traces are kept. \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\itshape Limits} \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_DISK\_HIGH\_PCT & 85 & Above this share of the disk, writes that would grow it answer 507. \\
QUEEN\_EPHEMERAL\_MAX\_BYTES & 256 MiB & The memory for ephemeral queues. Above it, their pushes answer 503. \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\itshape Security} \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_TOKEN & unset & The secret the nodes of a cluster share. \\
JWT\_ENABLED & false & Check a token on every request. \\
QUEEN\_ENCRYPTION\_KEY & unset & The key of encrypted queues: 64 hex characters, the same on every node. \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\itshape Upgrades and logs} \\
QUEEN\_RAFT\_CLUSTER\_VERSION\_MS & 1000 & \texttt{0} keeps the way back to the previous release open. \\
LOG\_LEVEL, QUEEN\_LOG\_JSON & info, false & How much the node logs, and whether each line is a JSON object for a log collector. \\
\end{options}
