Verdict. The new architecture sustained ~119k msg/s of balanced push and pop for 24 hours, processing 10.32 billion messages with zero push errors, an in-flight gap of 0.0005% (effectively zero loss), and flat ~399 MiB broker memory. This is exactly the long-run stability the previous architecture failed (it OOM-crashed). The throughput ceiling is Postgres CPU (~21/32 cores), not the broker.

3 · Storage & retention

One open issue — messages_consumed growth. The completed-message table reached 47 GB / 246,144,596 rows with 0 dead tuples and is still climbing (~2 GB/hr). The completed-retention reaper runs but trails ingestion by ~2–3k/s, so the table slowly accumulates. With 218 GB free that is a ~4-day runway — harmless for this 24h test, but it must get a working reaper before multi-day production runs. (WAL at 96 GB is the configured max_wal_size ceiling, not a leak.)
