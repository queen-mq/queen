Queen on OpenMessaging Benchmark

On one topic, Queen's native driver carries 1M msg/s on 100 partitions and on 10,000, a topic Kafka 4.3 could not create through OMB. With enough producers Kafka carries 1M too, with lower latency on less CPU. Kafka is far ahead on a single partition; Queen drains a backlog faster and keeps up with 1,000 topics.

Queen's native numbers include deduplication; Kafka has no equivalent. Every Queen message carries its own id, which the broker checks against a 60 s window and records on every push. Kafka's idempotent producer only drops a producer's own retries within one session, so Kafka does less work per message in every comparison on this page.

Three brokers on GCP (c4-standard-24-lssd, local NVMe) and three benchmark clients, 2026-10-16 and 17. OpenMessaging Benchmark at 5b1fa70: a 1-minute warm-up, then a 3-minute test, a fresh cluster before every point, replication factor 3. Kafka 4.3.1 with acks=all and OMB's kafka-throughput.yaml (1 MB batches) unless a row says otherwise. Queen: builds of its next release; its Kafka API is the same broker, driven by OMB's own Kafka driver. Latency is OMB's end to end, from publish to consume. Queues behind Queen's Kafka API have no dedup window, as a Kafka record carries no id; there, as on Kafka, the producers' idempotence applies.
